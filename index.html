Aahar Zen

Aahar Zen is a kirana-style e-commerce Android app for selling fresh spices, masalas, and coming soon multigrains.

The app is being built using Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Firebase to provide a smooth shopping experience for customers and easy order/product management for admins.

Core Features

  • User login and registration
  • Browse products by category
  • Product details and cart system
  • Checkout with Cash on Delivery and UPI
  • Order history and order tracking
  • Admin dashboard for products and orders

Current Status

The application is currently under development and testing, and has not yet been published on the Google Play Store.
