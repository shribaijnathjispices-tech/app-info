Aahar Zen

Fresh Spices • Masalas • Multigrains

Aahar Zen is an Android e-commerce application designed for selling fresh spices, masalas and soon multigrain products. The app allows customers to browse products, select pack sizes, add items to cart and place orders easily.

The application is currently under development and testing and will soon be published on the Google Play Store.

Core Features

  • Browse spices and masala products
  • Select pack sizes (50g, 100g, 250g, etc.)
  • Add products to cart
  • Simple checkout system
  • Order tracking
  • Admin panel for managing products and orders
